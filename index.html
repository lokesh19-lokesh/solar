CHAPTER 01 COSMIC ADDRESS

Where are we in the Universe?

The Solar System is our gravitational sanctuary within the Orion Arm of the Milky Way galaxy, orbiting approximately 26,000 light-years from the galactic core.

4.6B YEARS OF EVOLUTION
8 MAJOR PLANETS
290+ KNOWN MOONS

Formed from the gravitational collapse of a giant interstellar molecular cloud, 99.86% of all material coalesced to ignite the Sun, while the remaining protoplanetary disc accreted into worlds, moons, and rings.

CHAPTER 12 ORBITAL MECHANICS

HOW GRAVITY KEEPS PLANETS IN ORBIT

The celestial geometry of perpetual free fall.

01
THE SUN'S MASS
With 99.86% of total system mass, the Sun curves the fabric of spacetime into a deep gravitational well.
↓
02
GRAVITATIONAL ACCELERATION
Every planet is relentlessly pulled inward toward the Sun’s center with force F = G(M₁M₂)/r².
↓
03
TANGENTIAL VELOCITY & ORBIT
Because planets possess sideways velocity from the primordial disk, they continually fall toward the Sun but miss it forever—resulting in stable closed elliptical orbits.
